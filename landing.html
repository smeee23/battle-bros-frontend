A mystery worth studying. BROs are living organisms whose loose rock bodies are held together by an unknown force. The Biological Rock Orbiting Station (B.R.O.S.) was established to study them, and you are one of its researchers.

A caretaker’s work. Hatch specimens, respond to their Needs, train their abilities, and study their development through Forms I–VII. The station provides a controlled setting before you accompany them into the wild.

Two reasons to fight. Practice encounters in the endless world develop combat abilities. Ritualized territory challenges establish dominance and determine which BRO can occupy and defend a location. Each Bro occupies one location at a time; its defense remains active while you are elsewhere.

Beyond the station. The endless world is their natural habitat: floating rocky islands, distinct ecosystems, unusual plants, and wild NPC organisms. Exploration is your chance to observe your specimen in the environment that shaped it.
